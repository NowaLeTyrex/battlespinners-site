ONE-LINE PITCH
Build your spinning top part by part, then knock your friends out of the arena.

DESCRIPTION
BattleSpinners is a top-down pixel-art fighting game about spinning tops. Two to four players build a spinner from five parts, launch it into the arena and fight until one is left spinning, either by knocking rivals out of the stadium or by draining their stamina.

Every part changes the spinner's stats, and two of them grant special abilities such as dashes, black holes or stamina theft. A posture system rewards pressure: break a spinner's balance and your next hit becomes a critical. Solo players climb an arcade ladder against bots with eight difficulty levels. BattleSpinners is made by a solo developer in Toulouse, France, and comes to Steam in 2027.

KEY FEATURES
- Local multiplayer for 2 to 4 players, free-for-all or in teams; online play through Steam Remote Play Together.
- Five part slots with eight variants each. Every part shifts Attack, Defense, Stamina, Speed and Weight.
- 16 special abilities, two per spinner, tied to the parts you equip.
- Posture gauge: empty a rival's posture and your next hit lands as a critical.
- Timed launch: a charged launch gauge decides your opening speed and direction.
- Arcade ladder against bots across eight difficulty levels, in six arenas with their own hazards.
