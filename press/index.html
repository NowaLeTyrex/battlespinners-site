About the game

BattleSpinners is a top-down pixel-art fighting game about spinning tops. Two to four players build a spinner from five parts, launch it into the arena and fight until one is left spinning, either by knocking rivals out of the stadium or by draining their stamina.

Every part changes the spinner's stats, and two of them grant special abilities such as dashes, black holes or stamina theft. A posture system rewards pressure: break a spinner's balance and your next hit becomes a critical. Solo players climb an arcade ladder against bots with eight difficulty levels. BattleSpinners is made by a solo developer in Toulouse, France, and comes to Steam in March 2027. A free demo, including the story mode and Remote Play Together, is available on Steam from 11 November 2026.

Key features

  • Local multiplayer for 2 to 4 players, free-for-all or in teams; online play through Steam Remote Play Together.
  • Five part slots with 16 variants each in the full game (8 in the demo). Every part shifts Attack, Defense, Stamina, Speed and Weight.
  • 18 special abilities, two per spinner, tied to the parts you equip.
  • Posture gauge: empty a rival's posture and your next hit lands as a critical.
  • Timed launch: a charged launch gauge decides your opening speed and direction.
  • Bots with eight difficulty levels, and an arcade mode in Normal and Hard with horde fights.
  • Boss fights against the Sentinel and the Hive, in their own mode and at the end of Hard arcade runs.
  • Arenas with their own hazards: holes in the floor, bumpers and more.
  • A story mode told as a visual novel, starring talking spinning tops.
